Content Rewrites

Eleven patterns for search and AI-ready content

A working set of before-and-after rewrites covering the structural changes that most reliably improve both search performance and AI citation potential, with the reasoning behind each one.

Illustrative sample
01

Generic Introduction → Search-first Introduction

Before

"Content marketing has become increasingly important for businesses today. In this article, we'll discuss why creating good content matters and how companies can improve their marketing."

After

What is content marketing? Content marketing is the process of creating useful articles, videos, and guides that help customers solve problems before they make a purchase. A strong content strategy improves search visibility, builds trust, and generates qualified leads over time.

Why this works

The revised introduction aligns with the primary search intent by answering the user's question in the opening sentence. It naturally incorporates the target keyword and establishes topical relevance early, making it easier for search engines and AI assistants to understand and surface the content.

02

Generic Title → Intent-focused Title

Before

Different Ways Businesses Can Improve Their Digital Marketing Efforts

After

10 Digital Marketing Strategies That Drive More Qualified Leads

Why this works

The updated title is more specific, communicates a clear benefit, and aligns with how users search for actionable advice. It targets a long-tail query while improving click-through potential by setting clear expectations.

03

Weak Heading → Question-based Heading

Before

Customer Support

After

What makes great customer support?

Why this works

Question-based headings reflect natural search behavior and improve content organization. They also increase the likelihood of sections being surfaced in featured snippets and AI-generated answers because they directly match user queries.

04

Dense Paragraph → Scannable Content

Before

"Email marketing can be used in many different situations because businesses have different audiences and every campaign has different goals. There are several factors to consider before sending emails..."

After

Choose email marketing when you want to:

  • Welcome new customers
  • Promote new products
  • Recover abandoned carts
  • Share educational content
  • Increase repeat purchases
Why this works

Breaking dense paragraphs into bullet points improves readability and information discovery. Structured content is also easier for search engines and AI models to interpret, increasing its chances of appearing in summaries and list-based search results.

05

Opinion → Evidence-backed Claim

Before

SEO is one of the best marketing channels.

After

SEO consistently delivers one of the highest long-term returns among digital marketing channels. According to BrightEdge, organic search drives 53% of all website traffic, while research from First Page Sage estimates an average 748% ROI over three years for SEO investments. Unlike paid campaigns, well-optimized content can continue generating traffic and leads long after it is published.

Why this works

The revised copy replaces a subjective opinion with credible industry data. Supporting claims with trusted research strengthens E-E-A-T signals, improves content credibility, and provides evidence that AI search engines are more likely to reference when generating answers.

06

Keyword Stuffing → Natural Language

Before

CRM software helps businesses because CRM software improves customer relationships using CRM software features.

After

CRM software helps sales teams manage customer interactions, track opportunities, automate follow-ups, and improve customer relationships throughout the sales pipeline.

Why this works

The rewrite maintains keyword relevance while improving readability and topical depth. Modern search engines evaluate semantic relevance rather than keyword repetition, making natural language both more user-friendly and more effective for SEO.

07

Plain Text → Comparison Table

Before

Notion and ClickUp are both project management tools.

After
FeatureClickUpNotion
Project ManagementAdvancedBasic
DocumentationYesYes
AutomationExtensiveLimited
Best ForCross-functional teamsKnowledge management
Why this works

Presenting information in a comparison table makes it easier for users to evaluate options quickly. Structured data formats also improve content extraction by AI assistants and increase opportunities for comparison-based search visibility.

08

Generic Advice → Real-world Examples

Before

Businesses should personalize customer communication.

After

Companies like Netflix recommend shows based on viewing history, Spotify creates personalized playlists, and Amazon suggests products based on previous purchases. These personalized experiences improve engagement by making recommendations more relevant to individual users.

Why this works

Practical examples make abstract concepts easier to understand and demonstrate real-world application. They increase the educational value of the content while reinforcing authority through familiar industry references.

09

Generic CTA → Contextual CTA

Before

Contact us today.

After

Looking to improve your visibility in AI search? Download our free AI Search Optimization Checklist or book a strategy consultation to identify opportunities across your existing content.

Why this works

The revised CTA aligns with the user's stage in the buying journey by offering immediate value before requesting a sales conversation. This approach creates a more relevant conversion path and typically results in higher engagement.

10

Long Explanation → Featured Snippet Format

Before

There are several steps involved in content auditing and every company follows its own process.

After

How do you perform a content audit?

  • Export all website URLs
  • Review traffic and keyword rankings
  • Identify outdated or low-performing pages
  • Merge duplicate content
  • Update high-potential articles
  • Republish and monitor performance
Why this works

The revised format answers the query directly using a numbered process, making it easier for readers to follow and increasing the likelihood of the content appearing in featured snippets and AI-generated responses.

Bonus

SEO Content → AEO-ready Content

Before

AI search is becoming increasingly popular among businesses.

After

What is AI Search Optimization (AEO)? AI Search Optimization (AEO) is the practice of creating content that AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews can easily understand, summarize, and cite when answering user questions. It combines traditional SEO with structured content, clear definitions, and authoritative information.

Why this works

The updated version starts with a concise definition that directly addresses user intent. It introduces key entities, provides immediate context, and follows a structure that is easier for AI systems to extract and reference in generated responses.
